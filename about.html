Story

I came to design through photography. Film first. Looking closely at people, and learning to represent them with care. I stepped away from parts of that work for reasons of my own, but the way of seeing stayed. It is why the studio's approach is documentary. Honest before it is beautiful. Studio Sirad is where that way of seeing became a way of building brands.

Philosophy

Luxury through honesty. Minimalism that trusts the human story.

Location

Based in Aarhus. Working with clients wherever they are.

Background

Film photography, editorial and broadsheet typography, print production fluency (Pantone, ICC profiles, spot colour, CMYK), grounded in design theory. Built in Figma and static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Let's build something honest.
